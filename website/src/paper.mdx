## Overview

Asset2Sim investigates a shared pipeline from image-conditioned 3D generation to physics-ready assets and simulated interaction. The project compares geometry, collision representations, interaction behavior, and computational cost.

## Method

The workflow connects 3D generation, geometric preparation, collision construction, physical simulation, and robotic manipulation. Models and experiments share input manifests and evaluation settings to support consistent comparisons.

## 3D Generation

SF3D and SPAR3D are integrated into the generation interface, with TRELLIS.2 under reproduction. Generated meshes and their associated runtime measurements will be presented here.

## Physics Adaptation

Generated geometry is prepared for simulation with explicit coordinate transforms and collision representations, including convex hulls and CoACD components. Visual comparisons and geometry reports will be added as experiments proceed.

## Physical Simulation

Controlled MuJoCo contact and dynamics experiments evaluate the behavior of prepared assets. Experimental setup, media, and measurements will be added here.

## Robotic Manipulation

Robotic grasping and interaction experiments extend the evaluation to downstream tasks. Examples and results will be added here.

## Results

Experiments are ongoing. Tables, figures, and quantitative comparisons will be published here when available.

## Interactive Demo

A browser-based MuJoCo WASM experience is planned. The [Studio](./studio/) provides an entry point for future interactive demonstrations.
